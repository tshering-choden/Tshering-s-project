## 1. What is a Vinyl cutter?
A vinyl cutter is a small machine that uses a computer-guided blade to cut shapes and letters out of sticky plastic sheets.

![blade_tip](https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=18e0-WSAUAPe3V_KwLhFttjftlLYAanJg&sz=w800)

## 3. Processes of using the vinyl cutter:
  
I.To loosen the rollers, I lifted the top lever.

II.After that, I positioned the vinyl sheet evenly and flatly in alignment with the guide lines.

III.I then used the white pinch rollers to put pressure on designated areas.

IV.I chose the Piece option in order to choose the sheet size. (Given options are Roll, Piece and edge)

-I placed the vinyl in place and adjusted the wheel and then close the top lever.

## 5. How to use the software?

-To get exact measurements of the vinyl sheet on my Cutstudio software, I selected File > Cutting Setup >    Change > Get from Machine.

![Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 8.25.21 PM](https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=1pnj21yhIIcukJXEl_jEEpM8Nfqv_JutD&sz=w800)

- I then imported my design into Cutstudio. Then right-clicked on the imported image and chose the "Image Outline"  to create the outline of my image.
